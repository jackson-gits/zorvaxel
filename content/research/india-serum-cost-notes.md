# India serum launch cost article — research notes

Research date: 15 November 2026 (IST).

## Source 1: India supplier cost guide

URL: https://cleanbeautyindia.in/how-much-does-skincare-contract-manufacturing-cost/

The guide is dated 27 August 2025 and is published by Clean Beauty Private Limited, a manufacturer. Its table lists facial serum at ₹200–₹300 per unit with MOQ 300 units. It says the prices include formulation, manufacturing and basic packaging, but the facial-serum line does not specify fill size. The same page lists custom-formulation R&D fees of ₹5,000–₹20,000 per product and trial/testing of ₹10,000–₹30,000 depending on complexity. It gives examples of label design, packaging upgrade and domestic shipping in a simplified Face Wash 300-unit cost breakdown; those extras are not transferred as serum quotes.

Editorial treatment: present the amounts only as one supplier's dated indicative examples, not a market survey, guarantee, Zorvaxel quote, or direct per-unit comparison for a different fill size or batch. Product-only arithmetic for 300 units at ₹200–₹300 is ₹60,000–₹90,000. Keep custom formulation and trial/testing ranges separate; their scopes must be verified. Ask for a current, itemised quote.

## Source 2: Indian serum manufacturer service page

URL: https://www.swadeshlifescience.com/face-serum/

The public page offers private label, custom formulation, packaging and contract manufacturing, and describes MOQ as flexible. It does not publish serum unit pricing and directs visitors to request a quote.

Editorial treatment: use only as an example that a public service page can describe options without a public rate card; do not imply an independent price survey or market-wide pattern.

## Source 3: Non-India cost/MOQ guide — not used for India price claims

URL: https://gzcosmeticslab.com/blog/private-label-moq-cost-guide

A guide dated 1 June 2026 and updated 30 September 2026 states unit costs FOB China in US dollars, excludes international shipping, and describes other market MOQs. It is not an India-local estimate and is excluded from the article’s numerical claims.
